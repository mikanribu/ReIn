PROPERTY CATASTROPHE EXCESS OF LOSS REINSURANCE AGREEMENT

Contract Reference: CAT-XL-2026-001

between

ALPINE INSURANCE COMPANY S.A., Zurich, Switzerland
(hereinafter called the "Company" or the "Reinsured")

and

THE SUBSCRIBING REINSURERS
Helvetia Re (60%), Nordic Re (40%)

placed through
Meridian Reinsurance Brokers Ltd, London

ARTICLE 1 — BUSINESS COVERED
This Agreement covers all losses occurring under policies classified by the
Company as Property (Fire, Engineering and Allied Perils) in respect of
business written in Switzerland, Germany and Austria.

ARTICLE 2 — PERIOD
This Agreement applies to losses occurring during the period from
1 January 2026 to 31 December 2026, both days inclusive, local standard time.

ARTICLE 3 — LIMIT AND RETENTION
The Reinsurers shall be liable in respect of each and every loss occurrence
for the amount of ultimate net loss in excess of CHF 10,000,000
(the "Priority") up to a limit of CHF 40,000,000 each and every loss
occurrence. The overall aggregate limit of liability of the Reinsurers under
this Agreement shall not exceed CHF 120,000,000 in the annual period.

ARTICLE 4 — REINSTATEMENT
The amount of cover hereunder shall be reinstated twice, each reinstatement
at 100% additional premium pro rata to amount reinstated.

ARTICLE 5 — PREMIUM
The Company shall pay to the Reinsurers a premium calculated at a rate of
2.85% of the Company's Gross Net Premium Income ("GNPI"), estimated at
CHF 450,000,000, subject to a minimum premium of CHF 10,500,000 and a
deposit premium of CHF 12,000,000 payable in four equal quarterly
instalments on 1 January, 1 April, 1 July and 1 October 2026.

ARTICLE 6 — CURRENCY
All amounts under this Agreement are expressed in Swiss Francs (CHF).

ARTICLE 7 — CASH LOSS
The Reinsurers shall on request remit their share of any loss settlement
exceeding CHF 5,000,000 (cash loss limit) within 14 days.

ARTICLE 8 — CLAIMS NOTIFICATION
The Company shall notify the Reinsurers of any loss occurrence likely to
involve this Agreement where the ultimate net loss is estimated to exceed
50% of the Priority.

ARTICLE 9 — EXCLUSIONS
This Agreement excludes:
(a) War and civil war;
(b) Nuclear energy risks as per the Nuclear Energy Risks Exclusion Clause;
(c) Cyber losses as per LMA 5455;
(d) Pollution and contamination unless resulting from a covered peril;
(e) Terrorism as per NMA 2930.

ARTICLE 10 — SPECIAL TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately by written notice if
the other party loses fifty percent (50%) or more of its paid-up capital,
becomes insolvent, or if its financial strength rating falls below A- (AM Best).

ARTICLE 11 — ARBITRATION AND GOVERNING LAW
Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration
in Zurich under the Swiss Rules of International Arbitration. This
Agreement shall be governed by the laws of Switzerland.
