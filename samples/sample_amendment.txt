ADDENDUM No. 1
to the PROPERTY CATASTROPHE EXCESS OF LOSS REINSURANCE AGREEMENT
Contract Reference: CAT-XL-2026-001

It is hereby understood and agreed that with effect from 1 July 2026:

1. ARTICLE 3 — LIMIT AND RETENTION is amended such that the limit of
   liability of the Reinsurers is increased from CHF 40,000,000 to
   CHF 50,000,000 each and every loss occurrence. The Priority remains
   unchanged at CHF 10,000,000. The overall annual aggregate limit is
   increased from CHF 120,000,000 to CHF 150,000,000.

2. ARTICLE 5 — PREMIUM is amended such that the premium rate is increased
   from 2.85% to 3.10% of GNPI in consideration of the increased limit.
   The minimum premium is increased to CHF 11,500,000.

All other terms and conditions remain unaltered.
